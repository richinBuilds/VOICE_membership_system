Available Placeholders Use in Subject & Body

Insert these tokens anywhere in the subject line or body. They are automatically replaced with each member's data when the email is sent.

Placeholder Replaced with Example
{memberName} Member's first name Jane
{membershipName} Name of the membership plan Premium
{expiryDate} Expiry date (formatted) April 10, 2026
{daysUntilExpiry} Days remaining until expiry 7
{renewalUrl} Link to the renewal page https://voice.org/upgrade-membership
Email Subject Line
The subject line of the email. Placeholders (e.g. {daysUntilExpiry}) are supported.
Email Body
Plain text. Use blank lines to create paragraphs. Placeholders are replaced per member when sent. Leave this field empty to use the default styled HTML email template instead.
